How to play Wild Six

Play money only. Pick a table from the menu. Every game follows the same steps.

  1. Pick a chip: 10, 50, 100, 500 or 1,000.
  2. Tap a spot to place a bet. Tap again to add more. There is no maximum bet, only your balance.
  3. Press Roll (or Space).
  4. Wins are paid automatically, then the next round starts.

Buttons: Undo removes your last chip, Clear removes all bets, Rebet repeats the last round, Double doubles every bet. Keys: 1–6 bet on a creature, Space rolls, Z undoes.

Boost

Before each round one creature glows with a boost of ×2, ×3, ×5 or ×10. Each die that lands on it pays that much more. ×2 is by far the most common and ×10 is rare. It is the moon in Wild Six, the pearl in Twin Tide and the lantern in Triple Throne.

1. Wild Six (1 die, Halloween)

  • One die has six creatures: bat, cat, spider, owl, raven and toad.
  • Bet on any creature. If the die lands on it, you win 4 to 1. Your stake is returned with every win.

2. Twin Tide (2 dice, underwater)

  • Single bet (6 cards): wins if either die lands on that creature. Pays 1.6 to 1 for each die that lands there, so both dice on it pay 3.2 to 1.
  • Straddle bet (7 round buttons between cards): backs both neighbouring creatures. Wins if one or both dice land on either, paying 0.5 to 1 for each die that lands. Right-click a straddle to remove its bet.

3. Triple Throne (3 dice, royal beasts)

  • Beast bet (6 cards): wins if any die lands on that beast. Pays 0.9 to 1 for each die that lands there.
  • Triple bar (under each beast): wins only if all three dice land on that beast. Pays 205 to 1.
  • Any Triple (bottom bar): wins if all three dice show the same beast, whichever it is. Pays 33 to 1.
  • The win streak flame is just for fun and does not change payouts.

Examples

Twin Tide: you bet 100 on Clownfish and the dice show Clownfish and Jellyfish. You get your 100 back plus 160 profit, so 260 in total.

Triple Throne: you bet 10 on the Dragon Triple and the dice show three Dragons. You win 2,060, which is 2,050 profit plus your stake. Any beast or Any Triple bets you placed on the same roll pay as well.

Your coins

You start with 5,000 coins per game. Each game keeps its own balance, saved in your browser. If you run out, a button gives you 5,000 more.
